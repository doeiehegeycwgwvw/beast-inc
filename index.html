Pharmaceutical materials for student learning

Real medicines. Real study.

Beast Inc. sources, purchases, and supplies pharmaceutical products, reference materials, and educational kits so students can examine and learn from authentic medicines in a structured academic setting. Headquartered in Chiang Mai, Thailand, we support pharmacy, medical, and life-science programs across multiple countries.

What we do

Supplying authentic pharmaceutical materials for education

Sourcing & purchasing

We identify and buy quality-controlled pharmaceuticals, reference standards, and related materials, including Tyra-300, erdafitinib, and other oncology kinase inhibitors, from trusted suppliers for academic use.

Educational preparation

Materials are prepared, documented, and packaged so students can safely study real medicines in labs and classrooms.

Distribution to institutions

We deliver study kits and individual products to universities, training centres, and educational programs.

Our educational focus

Supporting learning across key therapeutic areas

Beast Inc. supplies pharmaceutical study materials across seven main areas, with a strong emphasis on oncology kinase inhibitors such as Tyra-300 and erdafitinib. Every product follows the same quality and documentation standards before it reaches students.
