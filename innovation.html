Science & Innovation

The fictional technology behind Beast Inc.'s research

A look at the imagined tools and systems used across Beast Inc.'s fictional laboratories, created for this educational project.

Computational drug discovery

A fictional modelling system used to narrow large sets of imagined compounds down to a smaller shortlist for laboratory testing.

Molecular screening

Imagined high-throughput screening equipment described as testing thousands of fictional samples for early signs of activity.

Pharmaceutical chemistry

Fictional chemistry teams described as designing and refining imagined molecular structures for further study.

AI-assisted research

An imagined research-support system used in the story to help organise laboratory data and flag patterns for scientists to review.

Advanced laboratory systems

Fictional laboratory facilities described as automated, climate-controlled, and built for imagined reproducible experiments.

Drug delivery technology

Imagined formulation science exploring how fictional candidates could one day be delivered more precisely.

How it fits together

A simplified, fictional discovery process

1. Imagined screening

Fictional compound libraries are searched computationally for promising candidates.

2. Fictional lab testing

Shortlisted imagined candidates move into simulated laboratory testing.

3. Imagined review

Results are reviewed by fictional scientists before a program can advance.

Built for a classroom scenario

Every system here is imagined for this presentation

None of Beast Inc.'s described technologies are real products or real research systems. They exist to illustrate, in a school business project, how a fictional pharmaceutical company might describe its research capabilities.
