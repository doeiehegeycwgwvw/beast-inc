About Beast Inc.

Beast Inc. is a mid-size pharmaceutical research company founded in 2018 and headquartered at PWFX+MR4, San Phak Wan, Hang Dong, Chiang Mai 50231, Thailand.

Our history

From a small chemistry lab to a regional research company

Beast Inc. began in 2018 as a small pharmaceutical chemistry lab founded by three researchers in Chiang Mai. The company grew steadily, opening research partnerships across Asia, Europe, and North America. Today it employs over 20+ staff across Chiang Mai.

Mission

Beast Inc. purchases and distributes quality-controlled pharmaceuticals, reference standards, and study kits so pharmacy, medical, and life-science students can examine, analyze, and understand real medicines safely and effectively.

Vision

A future where every student gains practical pharmaceutical knowledge through hands-on study

We aim to be the trusted partner that connects educational institutions with the pharmaceutical products and materials they need, helping the next generation of healthcare professionals learn from real examples rather than theory alone.
