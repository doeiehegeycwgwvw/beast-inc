About Beast Inc.

A fictional research company, built for this classroom project

Beast Inc. was imagined as a mid-size pharmaceutical research company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Bangkok, Thailand — created entirely for a school business presentation.

Our fictional history

From a small chemistry lab to an imagined regional research company

In this fictional story, Beast Inc. began in 2018 as a small pharmaceutical chemistry lab founded by three researchers in Bangkok. Within the story, the company grew steadily, opening research partnerships across Asia, Europe, and North America, and today it is presented as employing over 1,400 fictional staff across twelve countries. None of this history describes a real company.

Mission

To imagine medicines that could one day change how disease is treated

Within this fictional scenario, Beast Inc.'s mission is to research, develop, and responsibly advance new treatment candidates through rigorous imagined science.

Vision

A world where fictional research reaches patients faster and more safely

The company's imagined vision is to become a trusted fictional partner in pharmaceutical discovery, known for scientific rigor and responsible research practice.
